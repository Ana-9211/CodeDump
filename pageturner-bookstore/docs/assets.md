# Visual assets and covers (status)

The shelf is a layout. Its wood, covers and decorations are **temporary stand-ins** until real, licensed assets are chosen.

* **Covers:** a book with no `cover_url` shows a plain tile that says "cover pending". It is not artwork and is never
  presented as a cover. Real front covers must be matched by title, author and edition and recorded here with their source.
* **Frame, ivy, ferns, fairy, cobweb, key:** not added yet. The slots are `.bookcase`, `.plank` and `.shelf-row .deco`
  in `src/app/globals.css`.
* Allowed sources: CC0 or public-domain items only (Met Open Access, Wikimedia Commons, FreeSVG/Openclipart), with the
  licence read from the source for each file. Nothing is drawn in code.
